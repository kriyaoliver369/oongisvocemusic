Academic & Choral Standards

The Foundation of Musical Excellence

We merge rigorous vocal science with stage presentation, preparing musicians from foundational breath work to international concert diplomas.

International SCSM Accreditation

Official examination partner in Sabah for the St. Cecilia School of Music (Australia). Accredited graded tracks from Grade 1 through Associate, Licentiate, and Fellowship Diplomas.

Pedagogical Vocal Science

Directed by Fellow Carrey Oliver, instruction focuses on appoggio breathing mechanics, vowel acoustic resonance, vocal break smoothing, and polyphonic choral blend.

Stage & Performance Arena

Home to The OV Singers, our celebrated community ensemble performing at the Sabah State Harvest Festival (Pesta Kaamatan), regional choral symphonies, and state functions.

Curriculum & Certification

Coaching & Specialized Programs

Structured musical pathways ranging from accelerated ensemble performance to internationally recognized tertiary diplomas.

Flagship 13-Week

The OV Essential Choral (OVEC) Program

Structured 13-week choral acceleration covering diaphragmatic breath mechanics, IPA vowel articulation, solfège, polyphony, and recital concert preparation.

Duration:13 Weeks
Director:Carrey Oliver (FSCSM)
Certification:Oongisvoce + SCSM Recital
International Board

SCSM Graded Exams (Grades 1–8)

Progressive certified examinations under the St. Cecilia School of Music (Australia). Available for Vocal, Piano, Guitar, Strings, and Theory with diagnostic mock clinics.

Level:Grade 1 through Grade 8
Board:SCSM Australia
Pass Rate:100% Proven Record
Tertiary Diplomas

SCSM Professional Diplomas

Concert recital and teaching diplomas recognized globally (DipSCSM, LSCSM, FSCSM). Mentored covering 45-60 min repertoire recitals, program notes, and viva voce defense.

Level:Associate, Licentiate, Fellow
Prerequisite:Grade 8 or Equivalent
Format:Recital + Viva Voce
Vocal Benchmark

Program TAKSIR / Tokoh Vokal

Standardized singing assessment and adjudication methodology. Equips singers and adjudicators with objective scoring matrices, register balancing, and acoustic health.

Focus:Adjudication & Diagnostics
Target:Contestants & Adjudicators
Duration:6 to 8 Weeks Intensive
Performing Choir

The OV Singers Performing Choir

Sabah's premier community choir (ages 15–50). Rehearses rich Sabahan indigenous choral harmonies, polyphonic sacred literature, and contemporary stage arrangements.

Audition:Seasonal Intake
Ages:15 to 50 Years
Stages:State & International
1-on-1 Masterclass

Private Vocal Artistry Masterclass

Individualized vocal diagnostics to develop natural resonance, expand vocal range, smooth register breaks, and build lasting vocal health.

Schedule:Weekly 45 / 60 Mins
Mode:In-Studio or Hybrid Online
Target:Beginner to Concert Level
